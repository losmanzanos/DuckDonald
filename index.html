$5 from every item sold goes to Democracy Forward Foundation, a 501(c)(3) legal nonprofit that takes the Trump administration to court when it breaks the law. Lawyers are slower than tweets. They also win more.

Wear it somewhere people will see it, post a photo, and tag us. Or skip the shirt and give to Democracy Forward directly. Both work.
